# Phase 7 public methodology boundary

All F1–F23 and Phase 6 multi-axis/confidence definitions remain frozen. Public tools project their canonical metrics and states; no tool computes a replacement equation. Research modes change bounded recruitment/review/sensitivity work, never formulas. Sampling new retained candidates uses a deterministic hash order independent of performance; pending/excluded/conflicting candidates remain in the frozen frame. Small exploratory budgets cannot support universal or calibrated predictions. Relative outliers and provider T4 scores do not establish opportunity; low supply alone does not establish gaps. History, policy freshness, permissions and missingness remain explicit. F17 is opt-in sorting only, F18 is evidence robustness within its disclosed assessment scope, and creator fit remains separate. Country metadata and provider geography do not prove audience location.

## Eligibility conventions and uncertainty

The default duration convention is at least eight minutes, with six/ten-minute sensitivity versions. It does not define YouTube Shorts: explicit official surface evidence confirms a platform class; provider labels remain probable. Requested live details distinguish archived/current/scheduled broadcasts. Archived live is excluded unless a frame explicitly allows it.

Spoken English requires audio/speech evidence. defaultAudioLanguage is a lead; title/defaultLanguage/region are insufficient. Faceless means recurring human host is not central, not no thumbnail face, synthetic voice or stock visuals. Full-video review and scoped observed-channel coverage are separate. One opening cannot verify a whole channel. Conflicting claims retain a conflict state even when a stronger review supplies the preferred label.

Current-small thresholds (<50k/<100k) and calendar entry-age bands describe observed size/time, not success or creator experience. Account birth, first visible upload, first observed eligible long-form, first observed niche participation and first MCP discovery remain distinct. Earlier hidden/deleted content remains unknown. Pivot indicators neither prove ownership transfer nor automatically exclude candidates. Activity descriptors never assert permanent abandonment.

The hierarchy permits missing levels and multilabel subject/entity, promise, mechanism, format and spoken-language layers. Boundaries/classifier versions are frozen before outcomes. Known sister channels share documented groups; style alone leaves relations unknown. Canonical dedup preserves route/query/rank occurrences. Entrant frames need an entry landmark, never five/ten future uploads, high views or ongoing activity. These are research conventions and deterministic evidence rubrics, not validated growth predictors.

## Phase 6 evidence confidence

F18 preserves the fraction of comparable predeclared specifications with the
same generic decision. Separate ordinal confidence follows measured coverage,
source quality/agreement, history maturity, independence, policy freshness and
sensitivity. Neither output is success probability. Numeric rules and F17 weights
are explicit caller configuration, with versioned external normalization endpoints.
Missing binary evidence uses F22 bounds; labels, ownership and historic views are
never imputed. Weak conclusions receive the same quality assessment as positive
ones. Source financial/proprietary values stay outside decision and sorting.
See phase-6-robustness.md and phase-6-decision-contract.md.
