# Public research tools — Phase 7

Exactly 15 registered / 15 planned. All entries are callable and typed; none are stubs. See [tool contract](phase-7-tool-contract.md), [tool reference](phase-7-tool-reference.md), [workflows](phase-7-workflows.md) and [verification](phase-7-verification.md).

1. `youtube_niche_research` — Research a niche using retained evidence and the frozen eligibility, analytics, qualitative and decision services. Reuse a research ID or resolve a stored boundary/query; explicit advanced profiles create immutable runs. Quick/standard/deep are bounded research budgets. Stored-only by default; refresh is explicit and permission-gated. Returns multi-axis state and F18, never a success forecast.
2. `youtube_niche_discover` — Find stored taxonomy candidates and their official/provider discovery routes, weak cases and coverage. Candidate generation does not imply opportunity or rank by viral scores. Explicit refresh can collect bounded official search or documented NexLev similar-channel evidence.
3. `youtube_niche_compare` — Compare 2–10 researched niches across performance, floor, repeatability, entrants, breadth, concentration, history, production, risk and confidence. Reject incompatible mode/age/goal/frame profiles. No default winner; explicit F17 is separately labeled sorting-only.
4. `youtube_niche_outliers` — Inspect canonical candidate hits with internal F1 lift, absolute reach, baseline, ordinary floor, F7 follow-ups and F20 chronology. Provider outlier scores are separate context and cannot establish niche quality.
5. `youtube_niche_content_gaps` — Inspect evidence-gated content and packaging gap states with demand, supply, differentiation, feasibility and sample coverage. Low supply alone is insufficient demand evidence.
6. `youtube_niche_packaging` — Describe retained reviewed title/thumbnail mechanisms, composition, density, homogeneity and auxiliary similarity. Returns sample coverage and uncertainty; no CTR inference or forecast.
7. `youtube_niche_audience_demand` — Inspect separate public demand dimensions F3/F4/F8/F9/F11/F14/F19 and reviewed gap demand. No private overlap, retention, watch time or returning-viewer inference.
8. `youtube_niche_sustainability` — Inspect repeatability, breadth, momentum, older catalog, idea depth, sources, series and production burden with historical maturity. Descriptive dimensions do not guarantee longevity.
9. `youtube_niche_risk` — Inspect separate rights/reuse, platform-policy, factual/editorial, production dependency, creator capacity and policy freshness assessments. Returns unresolved reviews; no legal verdict or fair-use duration.
10. `youtube_niche_geo_language` — Inspect verified/likely/unknown/conflicting spoken language separately from provider/title/subtitle evidence, declared country and optional geographic context. Provider audience geography is an estimate, never official audience fact.
11. `youtube_niche_history` — Inspect retained F10/F11/F12/F13/F20/F21 and protocol/maturity states. Unavailable history stays unavailable; current counts never reconstruct the past.
12. `youtube_niche_watchlist` — Create, read, list, revise or disable local niche-boundary watch intent with target channels, metrics, cadence and expiry. Revision checks prevent lost updates. Definitions only: no scheduler or automatic monitoring.
13. `youtube_competitor_analyze` — Describe a canonical channel's retained official public statistics, eligibility, memberships, scoped channel outcomes, patterns, risks, provider conflicts and history. Niche aggregates remain separate context; unknown subscribers and ownership stay unknown.
14. `youtube_competitor_compare` — Compare 2–10 canonical channels descriptively by current size, scoped ordinary floor, breakout dependence, repeatability, observed upload chronology, topics, packaging, production and historical coverage. No objectively better creator or private analytics inference.
15. `youtube_competitor_watch` — Manage competitor-specific local watch intent for named channel IDs and desired observations/thresholds/history. Separate from niche-boundary watches; definitions only, without background scheduling.

`tool list` opens an in-memory MCP session and checks the exact names. The resource `youtube-niche://status` reports Phase 7, package/schema, completed engine/tool surface, key presence and provider limitations without HTTP. Watch tools persist intent only; no scheduler.
