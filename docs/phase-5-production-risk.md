# Production, declared creator capacity, risk and pilot runway

F16 sums task hours and cash separately for each explicitly reviewed production case:
research, sourcing/fact-check, script, visuals, rights, edit/animation, narration,
expertise, updates, revisions/overhead. Each task carries evidence references, reason
and local confidence. A timed pilot plus three contrasting estimates is preferred;
coverage flags expose whether these exist. Incomplete inventory is never complete burden.
Different cases/currencies are not silently averaged. Select production_case_id to
obtain one unambiguous hours/release value when several cases are present.

Optional creator inputs declare hours/week, monthly cash, currency, desired cadence,
skills, narration/animation/footage and team size. Weekly capacity equals declared hours
divided by release task hours before extra overhead. Monthly cash comparison uses the
explicit annualized convention 52 weeks / 12 months. Missing inputs/currency mismatch
produce unresolved constraints. Required skills/capabilities/team limits are checked.
Compatibility is a separate declared-scenario assessment; underlying burden is unchanged.
No profile produces burden plus unknown compatibility, with no assumed budget or skill.

Faceless evidence records reviewed asset paths and host/field/interview requirements.
A feasible faceless path may still have costly animation, licenses or verification.
Phase-2 eligibility is unchanged.

Rights/legal review is asset-specific and records dependency, clearance state and verified
license reference where supplied. Labels are low_apparent_dependency,
moderate_clearance_burden, high_clearance_dependency or uncertain. Platform policy
snapshots separately record official URL, version, retrieved time, scope and refresh.
Factual/editorial review separately records verification/correction burden, disputed
sources, identifiable-entity verification needs and volatility. No allegations,
safe-duration rule, legal conclusion, monetization verdict or universal safe niche is stored.
See [current official policy contract](youtube-policy-contract-phase5.md).

F23 requires an optional declared PilotProfile. Planned_hours = setup_hours + all
release task hours + evaluation_hours + overhead_hours. Planned_cash_loss = setup_cash
+ variable release cash + contracted overhead cash. There are no inflows, public-view
conversions, RPM/CPM estimates, ROI or income forecasts. Dated outflows use explicit
cash_outflow_at when present; otherwise they use delayed publication dates, visibly
documented. Peak no-income deficit and declared cash/hour limits are reported.

Each release matures no earlier than its delayed publication + requested age. First
and full K dates are separate. The research example gives 100 + 5×60 = 400 cash,
10 + 5×8 = 50 hours before evaluation/overhead; a 300 cash limit is exceeded.
Weekly dates day0/7/14/21/28 with age30 give day30 first and day58 full maturity.
Delays shift maturity, not success probability. Runway does not supply missing snapshots.
F7 needs a documented hit then five future eligible uploads; F19 needs ten comparable
attempts per entrant; F10–F13 repeated snapshots; F20 pre/post history; F21 comparable
multiple windows. Those prerequisites stay unresolved unless actual prior evidence exists.

Internal usage: analysis annotate --input annotation.json; analysis qualitative --cohort
ID --video-cohort ID --options options.json; analysis inspect RUN_ID. Python operations
are QualitativeService.annotate/analyze/inspect plus dimension functions. No HTTP,
heavy ML installation, public research tool, F17/F18 or final recommendation is involved.
