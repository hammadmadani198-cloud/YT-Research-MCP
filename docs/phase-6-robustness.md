# Phase 6 robustness and confidence

F18 is `consistent comparable specifications / comparable specifications`.
The numerator counts the same generic research state under a predeclared plan.
A finite-frame F22 bound crossing a declared binary decision rule is a reversal.
The denominator excludes unavailable comparisons; planned, unavailable and budget
omissions are all exposed. Zero comparable variants yields null, never 100%.
Material raw shifts and changed axes are reported separately even when an already
cautious generic state remains the same.

Neither F18 nor its separate high/medium/low grade is success probability,
statistical confidence, a forecast or a calibrated posterior. Repeating a biased
route does not improve coverage, ownership or source quality.

## Coverage, quality and agreement

Coverage counts unique subjects from the frozen frame, not database rows. Channel
coverage measures channels with available mature outcomes. Video coverage measures
available age-matched outcomes against eligible/uncertain stored videos. Topic
coverage measures classified videos. Each facet has its own denominator and
verified/provider-only/conflicted subjects. Audio metadata supplies coverage only;
spoken-language verification requires transcript or complete human speech review. Faceless channel review preserves the
actual reviewed-video IDs; a provider label cannot replace visual review.

Qualitative coverage uses each retained annotation's declared population/sample
IDs, with separate intersections against the scoped video frame. A sampled asset,
production case or premise is not a reviewed video or full channel. Materials,
material types, sample manifests, reviewer source types and inputs are preserved.
Timed pilots, estimates, transcripts, assets, sources and creator declarations
remain distinguishable. Comment captures expose video, route, ordering, date and
sample count; overlapping captures/themes are never summed as independent demand.

Field conflicts retain preferred source, alternative source and policy version.
Official, provider, human, model and creator sources are never averaged. Financial,
monetization and opaque proprietary conflicts are excluded by field; their IDs
alone may be listed to explain the exclusion. They cannot affect the state.

## Ordinal rubric

High requires complete declared critical coverage, verified classifications,
documented ownership, mature required history, human critical reviews, multiple
routes, no unresolved conflicts and stable required sensitivity families.
Critical insufficiency, conflicts, immature required history, stale policy,
provider/model-only critical reviews or sensitive/unavailable required checks
produce low. Other useful partial assessments are medium. The complete rubric and
assessment scope are emitted. This is an explicit implementation heuristic, not
an empirically calibrated cutoff. Confidence is symmetric: a well-supported weak
conclusion can have high confidence.

Caller omissions remain visible in required axes/history/families and ungraded
axes. A narrow declared scope cannot justify claims about omitted dimensions.
An incomplete budget appears partially tested/unavailable rather than stable.

History assessment retains the state, mature/pending/unknown counts,
numerator/denominator and full panel/interval details of F10–F13/F20/F21.
Available required metrics are counted separately from required metrics.
Provider history alone cannot meet the frozen official observation gates.
No current snapshot creates prior outcomes, stable protocol windows or history.

Policy freshness is assessed at the declared cutoff using retained dated policy
snapshots. Cached research remains an immutable assessment of that cutoff while
its dependencies remain permitted; later assessments need a later compatible run.
Absent/stale policy returns policy_refresh_required. Independent clock receipts
never restore expired policy content. No live browsing occurs in the engine.
