### Acquisition and verification manifest

- **Semantic routes:** industrial/transport/engineering discovery with max 50k subscribers; historical/science discovery with max 10k and max 3k average views; broader business discovery; dedicated weaker space/nature and industrial routes. No minimum-view threshold was used in weak routes. Dedicated constrained weak queries returned zero; this was recorded, not interpreted as absence of weak creators.
- **Live routes:** industrial history, space/astronomy, and ancient-history searches requesting recent/date/long/English results. Returned items included shorter durations, non-strict date ordering, some language uncertainty, and archived-stream indications. These are observed provider contract mismatches, not assertions about official `search.list` behavior.
- **Catalogs:** 15 channel about pages plus video pagination. The three established references and Micro are latest-30 slices; other routes reached terminal pagination. Primoral's fourth page completed its visible 107 objects. About/list differences were retained.
- **Exact details:** 47 selected video-detail records, including anchors, oldest-visible releases, and follow-ups. Provider index, catalog, and detail timestamps were not treated as interchangeable. Counts changed slightly or differed by rounding across surfaces; no velocity was estimated from these captures.
- **Spoken content:** transcripts obtained for `tXoLMmY7AoA`, `SsdfHbZYMNk`, `Hk05OO7l218`, `g5oBloiyU5Q`, `mw-UnwKOm5U`, and `2zOl31C5Kss`. Opening and selected middle passages were inspected. This confirms English speech in sampled passages and permits structural observation, not complete factual verification.
- **Visuals:** NexLev auxiliary multimodal analysis of the first 90 seconds of five videos: the same set except History Archive. It reported voiceover-led openings without a host in four, and a presenter in Bridged. These are model-assisted segment observations, not a human full-video/channel audit. They cannot establish asset licenses, AI provenance, production cost, or retention.

### Why the shortlisted findings could be wrong

| Finding | Strongest alternative / falsifier | Evidence that would change the design decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paired attempts materially change opportunity judgments | Existing outputs may already present exactly matched per-channel/per-upload/elapsed-time denominators | An implementation-independent output audit showing these contracts already enforced would merge C90 rather than add it |
| Post-hit ordinary change improves decisions | Apparent gain may be age mix, improved execution, event timing, or survivor selection | Prospective same-age panels showing no useful discrimination beyond existing floor/repeat metrics would remove F20 from recommendation use |
