# Content, premise, packaging and continuation evidence

Phase5-v1 runs produce separate axes. F15 is **validated distinct premises / generated
candidates**, with source-supported human-reviewed treatments, not a title inventory.
Canonical same/near duplicates merge; uncertain or contradictory relations suppress
precise F15 values. Same-topic/different-mechanism and same-mechanism/different-subject
relations stay distinct. T4 similarity never merges canonical premises by itself.
All premises use existing known in-boundary topic-family/video-premise nodes.
Missing reviews stay unvalidated; a completely unreviewed inventory returns unknown.
Ten human reviews and every finalist are research review targets. Thirty candidates
is an example budget, not a calibrated universal threshold.

Each gap review declares content/packaging/production scope. A supported gap needs
positive demand references, reviewed inadequate alternatives, differentiated feasible
production and checked credible accessible human-reviewed sources. Comments alone
can reach possible_gap only. Model/provider gap judgments remain possible, with their
source type exposed. No supply alone implies demand; served supply returns saturated.
Missing demand/supply/differentiation produces explicit states, never an opportunity score.

Saturation describes canonical similar premise pairs, independently reviewed publication
chronology and linked retained observed outcomes. Each prior run keeps its age, cutoff,
observation ID and declared goal; different ages are not collapsed. No copying intent
or causal performance claim is made. Prior Phase-3/4 results are immutable context.

Packaging reports title mechanisms, lengths/promises, reviewed face/asset/text-density/
composition/emotional-device patterns and auxiliary T4 visual similarity. The small
parser recognizes question punctuation and numeric text only; deeper mechanisms and
thumbnails require attributed review. Titles/media are not retained in annotations.
Public pattern counts do not establish CTR.

series_continuation_structure reports compatible reviewed premise links, structures,
finite/exhaustible concerns and finite graph depth. Cycles never imply infinite viable
ideas. Playlist-only evidence does not validate links or prove bingeing/retention.

Sources expose checked access/credibility, primary/secondary status, categories, freshness
and shared-source family dependency. Unchecked source availability is unknown. Checked
webpage count is not source quality. Source-to-story yield and unsupported premises are
reported separately from performance and feasibility judgments.

Synthetic Q1–Q15 fixtures live in tests/phase5_helpers.py and the qualitative tests:
Q1 limited ideas despite demand; Q2 broad inventory; Q3 repeated premises; Q4 absent
demand; Q5 supported gap; Q6 costly faceless assets; Q7 easy but weakly sourced; Q8 high
rights dependence; Q9 diverse sources/low dependence; Q10 high verification burden;
Q11 continuation graph; Q12 incompatible capacity; Q13 compatible capacity; Q14 short
runway; Q15 ten attempts without any income assumption. None is an empirical niche claim.
