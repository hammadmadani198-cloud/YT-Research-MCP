# YouTube policy contract — official review 2026-10-02

These current official pages were opened during development. Execution never browses.
Snapshot version: official-review-2026-10-02. Scope: YouTube monetization, GenAI
disclosure, advertiser suitability and copyright guidance. Internal re-review deadline:
2026-11-01 (30 days is our freshness control, not an official policy expiry).
Fixtures must provide retrieval date, version, applicable scope and expiry; old snapshots
cannot be used as permanently current policy.

YouTube describes generic/repetitive and reused content restrictions, with originality
and substantive added value relevant to monetization review. The current page also
addresses AI personas presented as human experts on sensitive advice topics. These
become review flags, never automated monetization verdicts. Copyright permission and
reused-content evaluation are distinct. [Official monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en).

Meaningful photorealistic GenAI alterations or generation involving people, events,
places or realistic invented scenes can require disclosure. Review records must
describe the actual production method and applicable scope; faceless or AI-assisted
production alone does not establish the outcome. [Official GenAI disclosure guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en).

Advertiser suitability depends on content and context, including educational,
documentary and news context. Our flags indicate a need to check the applicable
guideline, not predicted ad revenue. [Official advertiser-friendly guidelines](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278?hl=en).

Fair use is case-specific and ultimately decided by courts. Even short uses can
receive Content ID claims; no fixed safe duration is implemented. This model records
asset and clearance dependencies, not legal advice. [Official fair-use guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9783148?hl=en).

Policy, rights/legal and factual/editorial evidence stay separate. Provider RPM,
revenue, monetization status, quality and opaque opportunity scores are excluded.
Policy changes require a new annotation/snapshot and new analysis run, preserving
the earlier immutable decision.
