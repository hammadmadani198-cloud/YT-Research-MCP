# Phase 4 formula contract (written before implementation)

Authoritative local sources: Blueprint §28 F10–F13; Feasibility Matrix C04/C06/C11/C62/C63; C90 Plus Delta C91/C92 and F20/F21; Third Pass Gap Audit §L and formula amendments; Third Pass Evidence Ledger E01/E05/E08–E11; Evidence Ledger provider discrepancies and age-mixed examples; Updated Research Prompt same-age baseline/history requirements. Latest third-pass definitions govern.

All outputs describe retained observations in a selected frame. They are neither forecasts nor causal effects. OWN_HISTORY is exact official prospective captures. PROVIDER_HISTORY is auxiliary T2 and never enters canonical counts. CURRENT_SNAPSHOT is one point and cannot reconstruct history. RECONSTRUCTED_HISTORY is prohibited.

Shared constraints: UTC observed/available clocks must not exceed each declared historical cutoff; inputs must remain retained at execution and replay. Frozen cohort classification, boundary, taxonomy, eligibility and creator grouping must have been known by that panel's cutoff. Current subscribers, titles, index dates and lifetime totals cannot backfill earlier states. Missing and pending are never zero/miss. Numeric ratios use Decimal; raw numerators/denominators and counts remain in results. Sparse samples receive warnings, not confidence scores. Shared gates: available, partial_coverage, insufficient_history, insufficient_same_age_history, insufficient_panel_history, unstable_discovery_protocol, coverage_not_comparable, pending_maturity, source_conflict, policy_permission_required, provider_history_only.
